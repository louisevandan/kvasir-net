2026-09-15 23:26 KST. The implementation baseline P4 source is
`c16cbfa2abe8e7bc0bd7ce4f3e4c568f6a6c0569`. Verdict: **B2 GREEN; A-BYTES as a whole and

## Implementation and ownership boundary

`CompletionReservationGroup` was added to the backend-neutral completion mailbox. Before the first/middle
native call, the group reserves, once and as a total, the count/retained bytes of one forward plus the entire observation fan-out.
Each Event is split off as a claim no larger than its actual cost, and total usage is the same before and after the split.
Publication moves this claim into the same mailbox. A reservation failure, or an individual Event exceeding the limit, keeps the original group intact
and causes no change to the scheduler, flight, KV, native call, event ID, effect or output.

The llama.cpp worker's first/middle physical paths and the service observation, stage span and feedback are all connected to the same reservation.
No llama.cpp tensor, stage or model shape types were added to the common core. The actual storage cost is
computed from the owned allocation capacity of the `Event`, and is not replaced by payload length or queue row count.

## Failures and causes

`first_pass=fail`. In the first package regression, an existing owned ring fixture combined the 4 MiB completion store with a 512 MiB completion profile
injected around LOAD, and failed the new consistency check. The product path was not relaxed;
the fixture's store and profile were both set to the same 64 MiB. The physical result limit of 33 MiB and the request count of 16 are unchanged.
The exact ring counterexample and the whole package were re-run and passed, and both final workspace gates passed identically.

The first mutation draft removed the reservation itself and, as intended, stalled at full publication after native. After the root-cause location was
confirmed, it was replaced by a bounded mutation that only moves reserve after native, and the same counterexample was confirmed to fail immediately.
This initial stall was counted neither as a normal result nor as final mutation evidence.
