## Interpretation (F5)

Parallel execution is supported and scales, both on a single node and across the
distributed RPC pipeline: multiple sequences are processed concurrently (shared prompt
prefill + parallel decode), multiplying aggregate throughput. This is the batched/parallel
path of llama.cpp used unchanged over the cluster (micro-batches flow through the layer-split
pipeline). Small-model distributed per-token latency is higher than single-node (RPC
overhead), but parallel scaling holds.
